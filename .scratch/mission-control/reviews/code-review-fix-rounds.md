## Still open (none blocking; follow-up tickets)

### Should fix, not fixed and not decided

1. **mc 08 ST-2: two vocabularies.** `mission.py:112-114` defines `APPROVED`, `SKIPPED` and `SUPERSEDED` as a clock-judged "request reading". `approvals.py:30` uses the same names for stored statuses, and `Request.state` (`mission.py:123`) isn't in `CONTEXT.md`. Slot lists are still named `states` (`one_pager.py:97`), which CONTEXT.md, under **Slot status**, says to avoid. Failure scenario: a later change compares `mission.SKIPPED` with a stored status, or a stored status with a reading, and mixes the two meanings without any error. Fix: add **Request reading** to CONTEXT.md or rename the constants, and rename the slot lists.
2. **mc 08 ST-3: two switches over `db_state`.** `health.py:213-224` (`_database`) and `one_pager.py:60-67` (`_unavailable`) both switch on the same states. Failure scenario: a new state is added to `mission.py` and only `health` learns it. The one-pager then falls through to "isn't migrated yet" for a database that is in fact in the new state. Fix: one map from state to wording, beside the states in `mission.py`.
3. **mc 08 S-4: collapsing by slot count.** `one_pager.py:26,102` collapses any job with more than three slots in a day. PRD §5.4.4 (`prd-jura-mission-control-2026-10-25.md:917`) says "scout-send and publish as counts". Failure scenario: a publish day with three slots or fewer is listed slot by slot rather than counted, and a future job with four or more slots a day is counted. Today's schedules hide this. Fix: key the rule on the job, or amend §5.4 to "more than three slots".

### Fixed in part

It is the same approved email under the same idempotency key, so it can still reach the prospect only once. A nit.
